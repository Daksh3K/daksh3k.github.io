Health Insights

A personal tool that turns my own Google Health data into a weekly and a monthly health report.

What it does

I track my weight, activity and food with a Pixel Watch, Google Health and MyFitnessPal (which syncs my food log to Google Health through Health Connect). Health Insights reads that data from the Google Health API and builds two reports for me:

  • Weekly report: this week against last week and the four weeks before. It covers trend weight, rate of weight change, calorie intake, protein, steps, active minutes, resting heart rate and heart-rate variability.
  • Monthly report: the longer view. It covers total weight change, energy balance (calories eaten against calories burned, estimated from the change in body weight) and a week-by-week table.

The reports are emailed to my own inbox.

Who it is for

Health Insights is built and used by me, Dakshesh Gupta, for my own Google account only. It is not a public service: there is no sign-up, no account to create and nothing to install. This page explains what the app does because Google asks every app that uses its Health API to describe itself publicly.

What Google Health data it reads, and why

Access is read-only. The app never writes, changes or deletes anything in Google Health.

PermissionData usedWhy
Activity and fitness (read-only)Daily steps, total calories burned, active minutes Activity trends and the calories-burned side of energy balance
Health metrics and measurements (read-only)Body weight, daily resting heart rate, daily heart-rate variability Weight trend and rate of change; recovery trends
Nutrition (read-only)Food log entries: calories, protein, carbohydrate, fat Calorie intake and protein against targets

It does not request location, sleep, ECG, profile or any other Google Health data.

How it handles the data

  • It runs on my own computer. There is no server and no cloud database.
  • Data is downloaded over HTTPS and kept in files on an encrypted disk.
  • The data is never sold, never used for advertising and never shared beyond what the privacy policy describes.

The use of information received from Google Health API will adhere to the Google Health API Developer and User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
