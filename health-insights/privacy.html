Privacy Policy

Health Insights · effective 22 October 2026

Health Insights is a personal reporting tool built and operated by Dakshesh Gupta ("I", "me"). It reads my own Google Health data and turns it into weekly and monthly health reports for me. It is used only with my own Google account and is not offered to anyone else. This policy describes exactly what the app does with Google user data.

1. Data the app accesses

The app uses the Google Health API with three read-only permissions:

Permission (scope)Data read
googlehealth.activity_and_fitness.readonlyDaily step count, total calories burned, active minutes by intensity
googlehealth.health_metrics_and_measurements.readonlyBody weight, daily resting heart rate, daily heart-rate variability
googlehealth.nutrition.readonlyFood log entries (date, meal, calories, protein, carbohydrate, fat and other listed nutrients), synced from MyFitnessPal

The app does not request or read location, sleep, ECG, irregular-rhythm, profile, settings or any other data. It cannot write, change or delete anything in Google Health.

2. How the data is used

The data is used only to produce my own health reports:

  • combining each day's weight, food log, activity and heart-rate data into one daily table;
  • calculating weight trend, rate of weight change, average intake, protein per kilogram, steps, active minutes, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability and an energy-balance estimate of calories burned;
  • rendering a weekly and a monthly report.

It is not used for advertising of any kind, is never sold, is not used to decide credit-worthiness or for lending, is not used for research, and is not used to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine-learning models.

3. Where the data is stored and how it is protected

  • Local only. The app runs on my personal computer. Data is fetched from the Google Health API over HTTPS and saved as files on that computer. There is no server, cloud database or backup service operated by the app.
  • Encrypted at rest. The computer's disk is encrypted (macOS FileVault). This covers the health data files, the reports and the OAuth access and refresh tokens.
  • Restricted access. The OAuth tokens are stored in a file readable only by my user account. Nobody else has access to the computer's account.

4. Sharing and transfer

The only transfer is the delivery of the finished report to my own inbox:

  • Each week and each month, the report (summary figures such as weekly averages, trend weight and rate of change, not the raw data points) is emailed to my own address through my own Gmail account.
  • A scheduled task in an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) on my computer runs the report script and sends that email. To do this, the assistant reads the finished report. Anthropic processes it only to perform that task, under the terms that apply to my Claude account.

No Google user data is shared, sold, transferred or disclosed to anyone else: not to advertisers, data brokers, information resellers or any other third party. The only exceptions are those required by law.

5. Who can see the data

Only me. No other person reads the data.

6. Retention and deletion

  • The local data table is rebuilt from the Google Health API on every run, replacing the previous copy.
  • The latest reports stay on my computer until replaced by the next run or deleted. Emailed reports stay in my inbox until I delete them.
  • To stop all access, the Google account owner can remove "Health Insights" at myaccount.google.com/connections. That revokes the tokens immediately, and the app can no longer read any data.
  • To delete all data held by the app, I delete the app's local data and report folder and its stored tokens. If the Google account is deleted or access is revoked, I delete all local copies of the data within 30 days.
  • Deletion requests or questions can be sent to the address below. They will be completed within 30 days.

7. Limited Use

The use of information received from Google Health API will adhere to the Google Health API Developer and User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated before the change takes effect, and the effective date above will change.
